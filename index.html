turalstylist

turalstylist is a small private tool. Its owner uses it to manage their own YouTube channels, including When We Were Wild.

What it does

  • Sets titles, descriptions, tags, publish times and captions on videos the owner has uploaded to their own channel.
  • Reads view and watch-time statistics for the owner's own channel.

It uses the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API. It is run only by the channel owner on their own computer. It is not a public service, and nobody else can sign in to it or use it.
